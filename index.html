Good questions

Frequently asked

How does this work with the systems we already have?

Your existing player account, identity and bonus-wallet systems remain the system of record. Salesforce connects to them through secure integrations, so players keep one login and your teams see one connected view. Integration details are confirmed with KLC and its vendors during design.

What does it take to get started, and what do you need from our team?

We begin by aligning on goals and the first journeys, such as new-player enrollment. From KLC we would need business owners for loyalty, CRM and analytics, access to experts for your existing player and bonus systems, and your consent and responsible-gaming requirements. Slalom and Salesforce guide the rest, and we agree on scope and timeline together.

How will our player data be protected, and who controls it?

KLC owns its player data. Access is role-based and auditable, and consent and preferences are enforced before data is used for engagement. Security and privacy documentation is available on request, and specific controls are confirmed with KLC's security team during design.

Who does what: Salesforce and Slalom?

Salesforce provides the platform and product expertise. Slalom, our implementation partner, works alongside your team on design, build and launch. Roles and responsibilities are laid out in our proposal and refined with you.

How will our team be trained and supported after go-live?

Business users learn to configure rules, audiences and journeys themselves, and we plan ongoing support after launch so your team is confident running it day to day. Training and support specifics are covered in our proposal.

Will it handle peak moments like big jackpots and seasonal promotions?

The platform is designed to scale for peak demand. We validate expected volumes with KLC during design so the experience stays fast and reliable when it matters most.

How will you help us prove return on investment?

We agree on KPIs up front, then measure with test and control groups and one performance view across enrollment, engagement, retention and value. Your team sees results as they happen and decides what to scale.

What do the numbers on this page mean?

Every figure here is illustrative, to show how the experience works. This page is a post-presentation companion, not a product demonstration, and the screens do not display Salesforce technology. Screens, flows and numbers are examples for discussion and are subject to design validation with KLC.
